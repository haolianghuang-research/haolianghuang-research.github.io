AI & Robotics

Haoliang Huang

Embodied AI, VLA & World–Action Models
Agentic Manipulation

I study how robots can use visual experience, language, and memory to learn new tasks. I am a master’s student in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich, with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from KIT.

At TUM Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), I am a student research assistant working on path and motion planning for autonomous driving, including agentic planning and traffic-rule prediction.

At LMU TRESP Lab, I collaborate on integrating memory mechanisms with robotics, within the lab’s broader research on large language models, post-training and model memory.

At TUM Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, I collaborate on embodied AI research at the intersection of 3D computer vision and robotics.

Previously, I was an AI intern at Huawei Munich Research Center, working on CAN-based foundation models for vehicle dynamics and tire–road friction estimation.

My work spans VLA and world-action models, robot in-context learning, 3D vision, reinforcement learning, distributed training, and agent design for physical AI. I enjoy building agentic systems that automate workflows such as data generation and processing, and connect learned models to physical manipulation, planning, and simulation.

Looking ahead, I aim to integrate insights from VLA and world-action models, 3D/4D visual understanding, and memory mechanisms for LLMs and WAMs into these agentic systems. Together with my research on reinforcement learning and post-training, this work will build a foundation for future research on Embodied RSI.
