Haoliang Huang

I study Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), with a background in Computer Science from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). My work spans robot learning, vision-language-action models, 3D perception, and autonomous driving.

I am interested in connecting perception, learning, and control to build robots that can act in the real world. My projects combine hands-on robot deployment with large-scale data pipelines, distributed training, and careful experimental evaluation.
