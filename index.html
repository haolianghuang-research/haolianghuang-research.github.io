Embodied AI · World–Action Models · Agentic Robotics

Haoliang Huang

I work on embodied AI, with a focus on world–action models, demonstration-conditioned transfer, and agentic robotics. I study Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), following a Computer Science degree at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT).

My projects span model architecture and multimodal data pipelines through distributed training, robot/simulation integration, and controlled evaluation. I am interested in representations and interfaces that make complex robot behavior easier to learn, inspect, and improve.
