AI & Robotics

Haoliang Huang

Embodied AI, VLA & World–Action Models
Agentic Manipulation

I study how robots can use visual experience, language, and memory to learn new tasks. I am a master’s student in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich, with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from KIT.

My work spans VLA and world–action models, robot in-context learning, 3D vision, reinforcement learning, and distributed training. I build workflows for data generation and processing, and agentic systems that connect learned models to physical manipulation, planning, and simulation.
